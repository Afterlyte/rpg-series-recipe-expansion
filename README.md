# RPG Series Recipe Expansion
A datapack designed to expand the available recipe options for upgrading equipment in the RPG Series mods by daedelus_dev, for the purposes of seamlessly progressing equipment tiers while keeping enchantments and other custom NBT data within the items

Current list of recipe features include:
1. Ranger armor smithing recipes using Jade (Jewelry) and Turtle Scute
2. Assassin armor smithing recipes using Ruby (Jewelry) and Rabbit Hide
3. Berserker armor smithing recipes using Ruby (Jewelry) and Netherite Scrap
4. Crusader armor smithing recipes using Sapphire (Jewelry) and Ghast Tears
5. Prior armor smithing recipes using Citrine (Jewelry) and Ghast Tears
6. Elemental robes smthing recipes using their respective Jewelry gems (Topaz/Tanzanite) and respective upgrade ingredient (Ender Pearl/Blaze Powder/Prismarine Shard)
9. Diamond weapon upgrades for the Claymore, Dagger, Double Axe, Glaive, Great Hammer, Holy Staff, Holy Wand, Kite Shield, Mace, Sickle and Spear, using their class-applicable Jewelry gem (see above) and Diamonds
10. Elemental staff smithing recipes using their respective Jewelry gems and respective upgrade ingredient (Ender Pearl/Blaze Powder/Prismarine Crystals), upgrading from either the Wizard Staff or their respective Wands
11. Mechanical Shortbow and Royal Longbow smithing recipes from the Composite Longbow using Jade (Jewelry) and either Redstone or Diamonds
